December Hike Odds Surged to 81%. CPI Tuesday Locks the December Call.

The Quick Read

Over the weekend, December FOMC hike odds climbed from Friday’s ~75% to approximately 81% (CME FedWatch via Motley Fool, November 8) as Treasury futures absorbed October’s 165,000 NFP without a competing signal to blunt it. The 10-year ended Friday near ~4.84–4.87%; Sunday’s pricing holds that level. CD savers entered the weekend with three-in-four odds of a December raise; they now face four-in-five – the strongest reading since September’s hike itself. That repricing, with no new data until Tuesday, is the full mechanism entering Monday’s open.

Tuesday’s October CPI at 8:30 AM ET is the last inflation read before the December 9 FOMC. At ~81% hike odds, a benign reading near recent core levels confirms the path; a re-acceleration above 3.0% core pushes odds above 90% and closes the hold debate entirely. Monday’s November 9 USDA Crop Progress is the week’s first decision point – Iowa stood at 66% harvested as of November 1, 13 points behind the five-year average, with freeze risk arriving this weekend. A reading below 70% with freeze confirmed would reprice December corn futures upward at Monday’s open.

Gas wave #3 – locked from October 28’s $115.32 Brent – is arriving at retail stations November 8–12 even as the AAA national average edged to $4.04 (November 7) in the quiet before the wave lands. Wave #4 (from this week’s $87–$98 Brent range) arrives lower. The NY Fed Q3 consumer credit report (card delinquency 90+ at 12.9% last quarter) is due this month – the first full post-September-hike quarter read arriving against a 165,000 NFP backdrop.
