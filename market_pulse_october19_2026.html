AI Stocks Rebound, Yields Fall Monday Morning. Tesla and Meta Report Wednesday.

The Quick Read

Nasdaq futures are up more than 1% before the open after AI-spending valuation concerns drove last week's 4.5% loss. The recovery is led by chip names: Intel gained 4% in pre-market after a JPMorgan upgrade, and neocloud operators CoreWeave and Nebius rebounded on reports of a Nvidia credit lifeline to AI infrastructure customers. Treasury yields and oil are both declining - Brent pulled back from Friday's $109.7 spike to near $105.2, partially unwinding the geopolitical supply premium. Lower yields and lower oil remove two of last week's three pressures at once. The remaining pressure is earnings: whether AI-infrastructure investment generates revenue fast enough at current borrowing costs. Only Tesla and Meta answer that question, on Wednesday. That is the whole mechanism.

Monday's two market-moving events arrive before and after the open. The NAHB Housing Market Index publishes at 10:00 AM ET - the first builder confidence read since Freddie Mac's rate paused at 7.40% after eight consecutive increases. The USDA Crop Progress report arrives around 3:00 PM ET, covering October 14-18 harvest conditions that should show corn harvest pace well past the 33% reading from two weeks ago. NAHB shapes next Thursday's Freddie Mac reading; the crop report answers whether the corn harvest deficit is closing before the season's window narrows.

Bitcoin hit $82,753 at 6:00 AM ET - its highest level since last Wednesday - after holding the $80,000 floor through the Nasdaq's worst week since June and through weekend speculation about a "Black Monday" selloff that did not materialize. The FOMC blackout seals October 28 hike odds at approximately 15% through the week. That rate backdrop is unchanged from Sunday, and the 4.50% APY on 1-year CDs carries the same 15-day certainty entering Monday.
