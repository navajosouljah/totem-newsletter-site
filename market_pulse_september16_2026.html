CD Rates Rise on Tuesday's Hike. Today's Dot Plot Signals November.

The Quick Read

The CLARITY Act failed 49-50 at 2:15 PM ET Tuesday - 11 votes short of the 60 needed for cloture. Collins, Hawley, Moran, and Tillis voted no alongside all Democrats, closing the 2026 legislative window. Bitcoin fell 2.66% to $75,815; XRP fell 9.54% to $1.29. The Federal Reserve delivered a 25-basis-point hike at approximately 2:00 PM ET Tuesday, moving the fed funds target to 3.75-4.00% as CME FedWatch had priced at 92-93%. The mechanism: the hike pushes HYSA rates from 4.21% toward 4.35-4.45% within days, and 1-year CDs from 4.30% toward 4.45-4.60% APY as banks reprice.

What today decides is not the hike - that happened yesterday. What today decides is the dot plot at 2:30 PM ET alongside Fed Chair Warsh's first press conference since September 5. The median FOMC projection for year-end rates is the real signal: one hike and a pause through December puts the 10-year on a path toward 4.85%, which Thursday's Freddie Mac PMMS translates to approximately 6.88%. Two hikes - today's plus November or December - push the 10-year above 5.10%, pointing Thursday's PMMS closer to 7.10%.

Brent reached $109.21 on Goldman Sachs' estimate of three to five weeks for Saudi pipeline repair - the September 22-30 AAA reading is tracking toward $4.55-4.75 per gallon. Corn G/E held at 57% entering harvest with 8% of the crop in - the first stable reading after five consecutive weeks of decline from 65%.
