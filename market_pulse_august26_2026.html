S&P 500 Closes at 7,677 as Tuesday Rally Positions Markets for Nvidia Tonight - Brent Falls to $89.50 on Pakistan-Tehran Proposal. Jackson Hole Opens Tomorrow; Warsh Keynotes Friday at 10am ET.

Iran resolution signals shifted Brent from $91 to $89.50 in a single session. All that remains before Warsh speaks Friday is tonight's Nvidia Q3 capex guide - the one number that can break the chip derating or confirm it.

The Quick Read

Tuesday's S&P +0.3% to 7,677.28 and Nasdaq +0.7% to 26,151.30 were positioning, not resolution - markets rose because Nvidia's after-close report tonight is the only domestic catalyst before Warsh's Friday keynote, and Nvidia's +2% Tuesday advance reflects exactly that pre-earnings bid. The mechanism: Nvidia guided $91 billion for Q2; Wall Street consensus is $93-95 billion; the revenue beat is priced. What the Nasdaq needs from Jensen Huang tonight is Q3 guidance language - "accelerating" or "stable under pressure" for hyperscaler AI capex. A confident Q3 guide reverses Monday's chip derating (Micron -5.8%, AMD -3%+, Broadcom -2%+); a cautious guide hands Warsh a deteriorating Nasdaq as his Jackson Hole backdrop. That is the whole mechanism.

Brent extended its decline to approximately $89.50 Tuesday - down roughly 3% from Monday's $93.09 - as Pakistan's army chief concluded a one-day visit to Tehran carrying a sanctions-relief proposal. Iran resolution signals are now multi-lateral, not bilateral: Pakistan has emerged as a diplomatic intermediary distinct from Oman's formal ceasefire track. September FOMC hike probability has drifted toward one-in-three (approximately 33-40%), down from last week's 50-55%, as Treasury yields fell a second consecutive session and Brent's decline compressed the energy-CPI case for a September hike.

BTC pulled back to approximately $79,000 Wednesday morning in Asian trading - down nearly 1% as traders took profits after a 23% weekly advance. XRP fell harder to just above $1.44, down over 4%, as the CLARITY Act legislative-binary premium continues unwinding while BTC's institutional ETF bid reasserts the broader direction. The September 15 convergence - CLARITY Act floor vote, FOMC Day 1, Canada tariff day seven - is 20 days out. The crypto pullback is positioning compression, not a reversal of the institutional bid.

How Wednesday Opens

  • What Brent at $89.50 means for August CPI - the peak contribution math has already locked. The Hormuz standoff entered August 13 (ceasefire mechanism expiry) at a Brent baseline of approximately $88.31. The August CPI energy component captures the full month's average - which now includes 13 days above $90 before Tuesday's $89.50 print. Even if Brent returned to $85 tomorrow, August CPI's energy input is already running above the pre-standoff baseline due to the sustained elevated average over the first half of the month. The question is not whether energy adds to August CPI (it does, and the September 11 print will confirm it) - the question is how much the second-half-of-August decline from $93.09 to $89.50 offsets the first-half's $90-95 range. Pakistan's proposal does not solve August CPI; it changes September's trajectory.
  • What Nvidia must say specifically tonight - the language the Nasdaq is waiting for. The revenue beat at $93-95 billion is baked in; Nvidia +2% Tuesday was positioning around that certainty, not the beat itself. The market-moving variable is Q3 guidance language in three specific phrases: (1) whether Jensen Huang names Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, and Meta by name as customers "accelerating" demand; (2) whether Q3 revenue guidance exceeds $97-100 billion; and (3) whether Nvidia frames Blackwell architecture ramp as "supply-constrained" (demand exceeds capacity - bullish) versus "demand-moderated" (hyperscalers pausing to absorb inventory - bearish). The combined capex from those four hyperscalers runs $775-800 billion in 2026 estimates. A confident Q3 guide from Nvidia on all three dimensions reverses Monday's chip subsector derating before Thursday's Nasdaq open. A cautious or hedged guide on any one of them extends it - and creates the exact scenario Warsh would prefer not to inherit at 10am Friday.
  • Why hike odds fell from 55% to 33% and what that means for November - not just September. The shift from last week's 50-55% September hike probability to approximately 33% reflects three sequential data points: (1) Brent's decline from $93.09 to $89.50 over two sessions compresses the energy-CPI argument for an immediate hike; (2) Treasury yields fell two consecutive sessions on Iran resolution signals, validating the market's read that maximum-pressure sanctions accelerate a deal rather than a closure; (3) Tuesday's broad equity rally (-0 chip stocks from Monday's oversold condition) reduced the acute "Nasdaq deteriorating while FOMC meets" risk. At 33% September hike odds, Warsh's most strategic option Friday shifts: he no longer needs to hold the market at a coin-flip - he can deliver a conditional framework that preserves November as the more likely action window without pre-committing to September. The November 19 FOMC date becomes the new focal point for rate-path expectations if Warsh frames Friday's keynote as a data-condition speech rather than a September pre-commitment.
  • XRP at $1.44 and BTC at $79K Wednesday morning - two different risk trades unwinding simultaneously. BTC's profit-taking Wednesday morning (down ~1% from $80,227 to ~$79,000) is standard cycle mechanics after a 23% weekly advance that hit $80K for the first time since early June. The ETF bid that drove that advance - $1.67 billion in five-day August 18-22 inflows - does not disappear on a 1% consolidation; it simply pauses between allocation cycles. XRP's sharper 4% pullback to $1.44 is a different structure: it is specifically the CLARITY Act legislative-binary premium compressing as BTC's institutional-flow trade reasserts. When BTC is making a large directional move (either up or down), capital rotates from the legislative-binary trade (XRP) into the institutional-flow trade (BTC). The September 15 Senate floor vote has not changed; Polymarket passage odds remain approximately 27-30%. The XRP pullback is not abandonment of the September 15 thesis - it is the thesis on pause while BTC consolidates at $79K.
  • Warsh's specific keynote challenge at 33% hike odds - the conditional framework he should deliver and why. Warsh arrives at Jackson Hole with hike odds that have compressed from 50-55% to 33-40% over two sessions of Brent decline and diplomatic signals. At 33%, the September hike is no longer the market's base case - it is an upside tail risk. His keynote challenge is not to pre-commit to September (market would interpret that as hawkish surprise at 33% odds) or to signal a hold (market would interpret that as inflation capitulation given three concurrent supply-side CPI inputs). The optimal conditional framework for 33% hike odds: specify exactly what data combination restores September to 50%+ probability (e.g., August CPI above 3.8% plus August jobs above 150,000) and what data combination confirms November as the primary window (CPI 3.4-3.7% range, jobs near trend). This two-way conditional preserves both September and November optionality without forcing the market to price one or the other at an artificially clean probability. The test is not whether Warsh hikes September - it is whether he leaves Friday with market-implied odds in the 40-55% range for September and 60-70% for November, maintaining maximum policy flexibility through both remaining CPI prints before the FOMC meets.
