Brent Tops $100. PPI at 8:30 AM ET Narrows Friday's Call.

The Quick Read

Markets fell for a second straight session Wednesday - S&P 500 at 7,636.36 (-0.48%), Dow down 405 points to 52,380.66 - as Brent crude crossed $100 overnight to reach $101.25 in early Thursday trading, the first triple-digit oil price in this conflict cycle. The 10-year Treasury climbed to 4.85%, a new 52-week high. The mechanism: $101 crude flows to pump prices in 14-21 days, adds to Friday's CPI reading, and firms hike odds already at ~63%. Financial conditions are meaningfully tighter this morning than they were Monday.

PPI arrives at 8:30 AM ET this morning; consensus expects +0.4% month-over-month. Above 0.3% and hike odds likely move toward 68-70% before Friday. Below 0.1% would be the first soft producer-price print since June and firms the pause case. Freddie Mac PMMS also releases today at noon ET - with the 10-year at a 52-week high, expected range is 6.79-6.88% versus September 3's 6.71%. Friday brings August CPI at 8:30 AM ET and September WASDE at noon ET - together the week's primary resolution for the CD decision, the rate path, and the crop revision.

CLARITY Act Day 3 of 3 Senate working days opens today; Senate Republicans now expect the bill to fail at September 15 cloture after Wednesday's silence on the ethics impasse. Bitcoin fell to $78,219 - below the $80,000 institutional floor - as rate pressure intensified. Corn G/E held at 56% for the week ending September 7; Friday's WASDE is the formal revision from 180.7 bpa.
