Fed: All 19 Back Another Hike. Claims and PMMS Arrive This Morning.

The Quick Read

Wednesday’s FOMC minutes landed decisively hawkish – all 19 Federal Reserve officials supported at least one more rate hike this cycle, and the committee explicitly flagged upside risks from services re-acceleration. The 10-year Treasury surged from 5.18% to 5.31%, matching a 24-year high, as the S&P 500 pulled back from Tuesday’s all-time record to 7,801.77 (-0.22%). The mechanism: unanimous Fed agreement removes the market’s remaining hope that the September 16 hike was the final one, forcing a repricing of every rate-sensitive asset – equities, mortgage rates, crypto – simultaneously. That is the whole mechanism: hawkish unanimous minutes + 10-year at 5.31% = stocks retreat, BTC falls $5,500 in two hours.

Two releases arrive this morning. Weekly initial jobless claims at 8:30 AM ET (consensus 200,000) answer whether September’s 29,000 payrolls print was a weather anomaly or the start of a trend – above 220,000 confirms a deepening softening, below 200,000 points to September as an outlier. Freddie Mac’s PMMS releases at noon ET; the survey absorbed Wednesday’s hawkish minutes spike and the estimate has moved from 7.10-7.18% to 7.35-7.45%, which would mark the highest weekly reading since 2001.

December now prices as near-certain: CME FedWatch moved above 75% probability for a December hike after the minutes. Bitcoin fell from $89,000 to $83,500 in two hours on the rate-path repricing. September CPI on October 14 is the next resolution – above 3.8% year-over-year confirms the FOMC’s upside-inflation view; below 3.4% opens the first pause discussion.
