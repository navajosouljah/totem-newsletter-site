The 50bp Risk Is Gone. CD Savers Can Lock Before PCE Wednesday.

The Quick Read

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent’s Sunday morning interview on Face the Nation cleared December’s 50bp tail before the week opened. Bessent warned publicly against Fed over-tightening into a slowdown - a signal that, combined with Thursday’s 203K Claims overshoot already narrowing the aggressive hike scenario, moved December pricing from 25bp at approximately 91–94% and 50bp at 6–9%, to 25bp at approximately 93% and 50bp at approximately 0%. That is the whole mechanism: the CD wait-or-lock decision is now a base-case 25bp math problem, not a tail risk.

Wednesday, November 25 at 8:30 AM ET brings PCE, Q3 GDP second estimate, and Initial Claims - simultaneously the FOMC media blackout begins. With 50bp gone, PCE’s job has changed: a hot print cannot revive the aggressive tail, but it confirms the base case. After Wednesday morning, no Fed communication reaches markets until December 9 - 16 days away. The CD math at 25bp: lock at 4.75% now or wait 16 days for approximately 4.90% in January.
