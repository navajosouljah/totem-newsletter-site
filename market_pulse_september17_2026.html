CD Rates Rising Toward 4.50%. Housing Starts and Today's PMMS Open Thursday.

The Quick Read

Warsh's press conference landed harder than the hike itself. Twelve of 18 FOMC members wrote in another hike for 2026 - November or December - and Warsh's own dot was blank again as he doubled down on inflation language. The Dow fell 631 points to 51,461 Wednesday as financial stocks bore the direct mark-to-market cost of yields crossing 5%; the Nasdaq barely moved. The mechanism: each additional 25-basis-point hike means CD ceilings move again - 1-year CDs currently repricing from 4.30% toward 4.45-4.60% APY this week have a second reprice ahead, with the next ceiling near 4.60-4.75% if November delivers.

Two releases this morning answer what the hike decision left open. August housing starts at 8:30 AM ET give the first full construction reading with both tariff layers active alongside 6.76%+ mortgage rates - July's 1,239,000 SAAR was already a multi-year low. The Freddie Mac PMMS at 12 PM ET, capturing trading through Wednesday's 10-year close at 5.016%, is expected in the 6.97-7.10% range - potentially the first 7% reading since October 2023.

Gold bounced to $4,461 Thursday morning, recovering 3.5% from Wednesday's post-Warsh low of $4,310. XRP broke below $1 for the first time since November 2024. The two moves read differently: gold is pricing the persistent inflation premium the dot plot confirmed; XRP is repricing 12 to 16 months of regulatory delay after the CLARITY failure.
