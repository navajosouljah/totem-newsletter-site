Brent Near $99. CPI Friday Settles the CD and Rate Question.

The Quick Read

S&P 500 closed Tuesday at 7,673.52, down 0.58% - the Dow fell 627 points to 52,786.07 as energy-linked industrials absorbed crude's cost headwind more directly than the Nasdaq, which fell just 0.32% to 26,421.41. Overnight the escalation shifted in character: US Central Command struck five IRGC-linked tankers, including one near Kharg Island - Iran's main crude export terminal - and Iran's Supreme National Security Council declared a formal "prohibited zone" in the Persian Gulf outside the Strait of Hormuz. Houthis simultaneously struck Saudi Aramco facilities with ballistic missiles and drones. Brent opened Wednesday at $99.12 (+1.23%), WTI at $94.01. The mechanism is no longer just a transit chokepoint - it is a formal boundary declaration adjacent to Iran's production infrastructure, a qualitatively different escalation than the prior tanker strikes.

PPI arrives Thursday September 10 at 8:30 AM ET. August CPI arrives Friday September 11 at 8:30 AM ET and WASDE at noon ET. Hike odds sit at approximately 58-60% (CME FedWatch). The FOMC quiet period is in effect through September 17. Tuesday evening's USDA Crop Progress showed corn G/E fell to 56% from 57% - the fourth consecutive weekly decline from 65% in mid-August. The drop shifts the WASDE revision range toward 173-176 bpa from the prior 175-177 bpa, narrowing the gap toward Pro Farmer's 173.2 bpa floor. Friday's CPI above 3.3% puts September 15 hike near-certain; below 3.0% flips the CD-lock decision.

Canadian counter-tariffs took effect September 8 - 15-50% on 700-plus items including lumber, plywood, doors, and cement - adding to the housing supply-chain cost stack alongside Day 17 of the US 50% softwood tariff. Bitcoin held its $79,000 floor overnight despite a brief dip below $80,000; BlackRock's IBIT pulled in $175 million Wednesday morning, sustaining the institutional floor even as BTC sits $1,166 below its September 4 peak. CLARITY Act Day 2 of 3 Senate working days opens today - September 8, 9, and 10 - before the September 14 return session and September 15 cloture at 2:15 PM ET.
