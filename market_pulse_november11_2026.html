CDs Climbed to 5.05%. WASDE Cut Corn Yield – Futures Rose 12 Cents.

The Quick Read

October CPI landed hotter than expected – headline +0.4% MoM (above 0.3% forecast) and core +0.3% MoM (above 0.2% consensus) – hitting the specific threshold Governor Waller said would put 50 basis points on the table for December. December hike odds moved to approximately 100%; the 50bp probability rose from 0% to approximately 10%. The 10-year yield jumped to 4.98%, S&P 500 gave back 0.38% of Monday’s 2.35% recovery to close at 7,520.93. USDA WASDE cut corn yield from 181.2 to 179.6 BPA, driving corn futures up 12+ cents on a bullish supply surprise. That is the full mechanism entering Wednesday.

Today is Veterans Day – bond market closed, equities open, no major US economic releases. The bond market’s closure means the 10-year’s 4.98% close from Tuesday is the last reading until Thursday. Thursday brings two catalysts: initial jobless claims at 8:30 AM ET (prior 189,000, a 57-year low) and Freddie Mac’s PMMS (the November 9–13 survey week opened with the 10-year above October’s level that produced 7.24%). The improvement streak’s fourth consecutive week is at risk.

Best 1-year CDs are now at 5.05% APY (Fortune, November 9). With December at approximately 100% hike certainty, a 25bp move adds approximately 25bp to January CD rates (~5.30%); a 50bp move – at ~10% probability after core CPI’s third consecutive 0.3% month – adds ~50bp (~5.55%). Crude oil fell sharply as Iran risk premium unwound: Brent from ~$97.57 Monday to ~$91.92 Tuesday, making wave #4 gas (arriving November 15–19) cheaper than wave #3’s October 28 lock.
