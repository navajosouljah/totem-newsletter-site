Market Pulse • Sunday, November 15, 2026

Gas Drops to $3.82 Before Thanksgiving. Nvidia and Retail Sales Decide Tuesday.

The Quick Read

Pump prices kept falling over the weekend while crypto did the opposite. The AAA national average reached $3.82 Sunday, down from about $4.33 a month ago, as cheaper October crude works through to stations. Bitcoin touched $62,081 Sunday on $1.96 billion in liquidations and seven straight sessions of ETF outflows. Both moves trace to one source: a strong dollar and a near-certain December hike. That is the mechanism.

Tuesday decides the week. Retail Sales at 8:30 AM ET shows whether consumers are still spending into the holidays, and Nvidia reports after the close - its first quarter since Jensen Huang stepped down, with the stock down about 20% in November. A hot Retail Sales print pushes the 13% chance of a 50bp December hike higher; a soft one keeps 25bp at about 87%.

Underneath, the Fed’s communication window is closing. October’s FOMC minutes land Wednesday at 2:00 PM ET, Anthropic is set to begin trading Thursday, and the Fed’s media blackout starts November 25 - 10 days out. After that, the December 9 decision rides on data already printed. Curated from AAA · CoinDesk · CoinGlass · Farside Investors · CNBC · CME FedWatch · Federal Reserve

Gas fell again over the weekend to $3.82 (AAA, November 15), the fourth day below $4 since November 12. Crude closed Friday with Brent at $95.84, its second straight weekly loss on a growing supply surplus and higher OPEC+ output. Iran said Saturday that three tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz; the US military disputes the claim, and futures price it when they reopen Sunday evening.

Brent crude (Friday November 13 settle – Reuters / FinanceFeeds): $95.84/barrel ▼ second straight weekly lossBrent is down from a late-October peak near $118 on a growing supply surplus, higher OPEC+ output and rising US inventories (Reuters).
WTI crude (Friday November 13 settle – FXStreet): ~$88.56/barrel ▼ weekly lossStations sell fuel bought weeks earlier, so Friday’s settle reaches retail prices in early December.
Hormuz mine claim (Saturday November 14 – FinanceFeeds / Oilprice.com): 3 tankers claimed disputed by US militaryPresident Trump said a deal with Iran is not needed and the blockade stays. If the mine claim holds up when futures reopen, it adds a risk premium to December pump prices.
National gas average (AAA, Sunday November 15): $3.824/gallon ▼ from $3.841 SatThe national average first fell below $4 on November 12, its first time since July. A 15-gallon fill now costs about $7.60 less than a month ago, and the fuel at stations through Thanksgiving was priced when Brent sat near $92.
Bottom line

Gas at $3.82 should keep easing through Thanksgiving because it reflects October’s cheaper crude. Watch the Hormuz claim at Sunday evening’s futures open; it affects December pump prices, not this week’s.
