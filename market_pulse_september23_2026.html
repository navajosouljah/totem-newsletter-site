Nasdaq at a Second Record. Xi Arrives as WTI Falls Below $90.

The Quick Read

WTI crude closed at $89.85 Tuesday - its first close below $90 since before the September 11 disruption peak - as Xi Jinping's delegation departed Beijing for Washington and the geopolitical premium that had pushed crude above $105 just 12 days earlier continued repricing. The Nasdaq reached a second consecutive record at 27,244.18 on the same session while the Dow fell 185 points to 51,863.83, as the 10-year Treasury edged back to approximately 4.97%, pressuring rate-sensitive financial and housing names. That split is the mechanism: tech outperforms when summit optimism is bid; the Dow lags when the 10-year holds near 5%.

Xi arrives Washington today. The Trump-Xi bilateral is scheduled for Thursday September 24 at the White House - the first time a Chinese leader has met a sitting US president in Washington since 2015 - and covers trade, Taiwan, and AI export controls. A tariff-truce signal Thursday is directly positive for semiconductors, copper, and the Nasdaq; a breakdown reverses the current optimism premium. PCE inflation on Friday September 25 at 8:30 AM ET is the week's pivotal scheduled data: core PCE consensus at approximately +0.21% month-over-month. At or above +0.3% MoM near-locks November; below +0.2% starts softening the November probability from 60-65%. The Freddie Mac PMMS releases Thursday - with the 10-year at 4.97%, the threshold on whether it crosses 7% is a close call.

Two slow accumulations. The 1-year CD window enters Day 8 at 4.50% APY today - the window closes approximately September 26-28, and PCE Friday lands before it closes. And December corn futures rose to $5.44 Tuesday - a three-year high - as early yield data from the first Corn Belt harvest fields came in below the USDA's September WASDE estimate of 178.5 bushels per acre.
