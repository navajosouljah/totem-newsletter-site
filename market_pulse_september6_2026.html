Hike Odds Reach 70% After the Jobs Beat. Three Rate Decisions Land Before September 15.

The Quick Read

After Friday's 162,000-job beat, CME FedWatch's September rate-hike probability climbed from 52.6% Friday to approximately 70% over the Labor Day weekend - the highest reading since late August. The mechanism is direct: 70% makes the September 15 hike the base case for the first time this cycle. Banks will likely raise CD rates after September 15, mortgage rates will not fall before then, and the 22.18% average card APR faces a clear-majority probability of another 25-basis-point increase. Every rate-linked product now tracks September 15 as the base-case event rather than a coin flip.

Tuesday's first post-Labor Day session also absorbs a new military development. US CENTCOM struck three Iranian tankers Saturday September 5 - M/T Kylo sank in the Gulf of Oman, M/T Downy and M/T Stark 1 disabled near Kharg Island - after Iran launched ballistic missiles at two US Navy ships. Both ships evaded; no American casualties. The 10-year Treasury enters the week at 4.823%, with both 70% hike odds and a three-tanker Hormuz escalation to price when CME futures reopen Sunday evening at 5:00 PM CT.

The Strait of Hormuz remains closed - six vessels per day transiting versus 85 normally, a count unchanged for two weeks. Bitcoin held near $79,900 Sunday morning with the CLARITY Act entering its final Senate window (September 8-10) before the September 15 cloture vote at roughly 20% Polymarket odds. The week's sequence: CLARITY working days September 8-10, Freddie Mac PMMS Thursday September 10, August CPI at 8:30 AM ET and September WASDE at noon on Friday September 11. Everything settles September 15.
