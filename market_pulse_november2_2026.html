Oil Falls 4% on Iran Talks – Gas Relief Ahead. Futures Rise.

The Quick Read

Oil fell nearly 4% Monday morning – Brent to approximately $109.75, WTI to approximately $100.69 – as US-Iran diplomatic talks in Doha entered a third consecutive day with signals of progress. Trump’s stated preference for diplomacy reduced the conflict risk premium embedded in crude since October’s tanker strike escalation, sending index futures higher: S&P 500 contracts up approximately 0.5% to 7,650, Nasdaq-100 up approximately 0.8%. The mechanism: lower crude lowers the projected ceiling for gas wave #3 – from October 28’s $115.32 Brent close, arriving November 8–12 – without canceling wave #2, which is already in the refinery pipeline and arrives at retail pumps this week. That is the mechanism.

Two economic releases today set the picture before Tuesday’s elections. ISM Manufacturing PMI for October arrives at 10:00 AM ET – September’s reading was 54.5, the ninth consecutive expansionary month; a miss below 52 would extend last week’s AI-hardware growth concern into the broader economy. USDA Crop Progress for the week ending November 1 arrives at approximately 4:00 PM ET – the last read on Iowa corn, entering at 47% harvested and 20 points behind average, before northern freeze risk rises. Apple and Palantir report after the close – the first post-AI-selloff mega-cap earnings prints.

Gas wave #2 is arriving at pumps this week regardless of today’s crude drop; wave #3 follows November 8–12. December hike odds trimmed to approximately 72–76% from 80% on ECI wages at 3.1% year-over-year – the one-in-four hold scenario now carries real weight. Bitcoin’s 200-day moving average near $76,000 held Friday’s Binance liquidation event; tomorrow’s Senate elections are the next CLARITY Act checkpoint for crypto ETF flows.
