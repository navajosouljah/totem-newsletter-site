Market Pulse • Monday, October 12, 2026

Bank Earnings Start Tomorrow. Savers Lock 5.00% Before October 28.

The Quick Read

Gulf oil platforms began restarting overnight after Hurricane Isaias passed the Mississippi coast. Brent ticked up to $105.68 Monday morning - not on hurricane damage, but on Middle East tanker risk that persisted through the weekend. Stocks hold near all-time highs from Friday's S&P 7,811.54 close, but the real test starts Tuesday: four major banks report before the open against a backdrop of 12.9% credit card delinquencies and 7.40% mortgages. Those loan-loss provisions are the consumer's balance sheet read in public, for the first time this rate cycle.

JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Citigroup, and Wells Fargo report premarket Tuesday; Morgan Stanley, Bank of America, and September CPI at 8:30 AM ET follow Wednesday. The CPI consensus is 3.6% headline, up from 3.4% in August, and 2.5% core. That is the last inflation print before the October 27-28 FOMC meeting. Futures price an 82% chance of a hold - that number does not move on a 3.6% print. What moves is December: a reading above 3.7% pushes December hike odds above the current 78%.

South Korea's KOSPI fell 3.38% Monday on a Samsung earnings miss - the sharpest single-session drop in the region this month. Japan's Nikkei is closed today for Health-Sports Day. Columbus Day keeps US bond markets shut, holding Treasury yields at Friday's 5.24% 10-year until Tuesday opens trading. The week's rate story is deferred by one day, not one week. Bottom line
