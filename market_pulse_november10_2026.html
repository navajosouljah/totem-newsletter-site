Markets Surged Monday. CPI at 8:30 AM ET Closes the December Call.

The Quick Read

Stocks staged their sharpest single-day recovery in months on Monday – S&P 500 up 2.35% to 7,549.94, Dow up 1,104 points to 52,389.75, Nasdaq up 2.74% to 25,253.14 – as AI regulation fears from the Texas data-center moratorium retreated to campaign-promise stage and semiconductors led the comeback. AMD surged 13%; Nvidia fell 4% on a board investigation. Last week’s 3.9%–4.5% sell-offs flushed enough sellers that buyers stepped in aggressively on no new negative catalysts. That is the full mechanism entering Tuesday’s open.

October CPI at 8:30 AM ET is the last inflation read before the December 9 FOMC. Fed Governor Waller said Monday he backs a December hike regardless of today’s CPI result – and that a 50-basis-point move should be considered if core runs hot. December hike odds sit at approximately 96% (CME FedWatch) entering Tuesday. Hold probability: ~4%. At 0.2% core consensus (MoM), a benign print holds the 25bp path; above 0.3% raises the 50bp conversation Waller started. USDA WASDE at 12:00 PM ET closes 2026’s final corn supply accounting.

Iowa corn reached 72% harvested as of November 8 – up 6 points from last week but still 17 points behind the 89% five-year average. Drier conditions are forecast; Secretary Naig says finishing is possible. CD savers face a changed calculus: at 96% hike odds, the December question is 25 or 50 basis points, not hike or hold – and Waller’s Monday speech put the larger move on the table for the first time.
