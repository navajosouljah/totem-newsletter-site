Banks Already Moved CDs to 4.60%. FOMC Decides Wednesday.

The Quick Read

The S&P 500 closed Friday at 7,656.98, capping a week in which hot core CPI printed 3.4% - locking September hike odds at 82% on CME FedWatch - and four consecutive sessions of equity gains absorbed that news without a rout. The mechanism: hot CPI tells the market that rates are rising Wednesday; the market is pricing it in, not fleeing it. Banks already moved - top 1-year CD rates reached 4.60% ahead of the decision, 30 basis points above where they stood two weeks ago.

The next 72 hours decides two things. Tuesday September 15 at 2:15 PM ET: CLARITY Act cloture - seven Democratic votes needed on a 60-vote threshold, passage odds at approximately 20%. Wednesday September 16 at approximately 2:00 PM ET: the FOMC decision at 82% hike probability, followed by the dot plot and press conference at 2:30 PM ET. The CD decision window narrows Wednesday: banks pre-moved to 4.60% and savers who wait for the FOMC confirmation will buy into a rate the market has already priced.

Brent settled Friday at $104.61 - up 8.7% for the week on Iran prohibited-zone pressure and Houthi-Aramco strikes. WTI crossed $100 for the first time in this cycle. Gas at $4.31 per gallon reflects last week's $99-$104 crude; the pump window carries this week's $104+ Brent to retail in 14-21 days. Canada's counter-tariffs on housing materials are at Day 21 alongside the US 50% softwood tariff.

Japan (Nikkei 225, Friday September 12 close): 64,011 ▼ −1.93% (−1,261 pts)The Nikkei's 1.93% Friday decline reversed two weeks of AI-semiconductor-led gains. Yen strength to 152 per dollar - from 153 earlier in the week - directly compresses the yen-converted value of Japan's dollar-denominated export earnings. SoftBank and semiconductor names led the decline. The same copper-infrastructure theme that drove earlier gains (Furukawa Electric up 12.71% September 9) is not insulating Japan's export sector from yen strength. Monday's Asian session enters with the yen at 152 and the FOMC meeting opening simultaneously.
Hang Seng (Friday September 12 close): 24,805 ▼ −0.60%The Hang Seng extended its week-long decline as Brent at $104.61 settled Friday. Hong Kong's dollar peg to the USD means the 82% hike risk compounds with crude's approach to $105. Financial stocks, logistics, and energy-cost-sensitive manufacturing led the decline. Mainland China's CSI 300 moved in sympathy. The Hang Seng is approaching 24,000 - the support level that held through the July-August period. Monday's session opens with both the FOMC meeting starting and the first post-$104 Brent pricing.
Yen / Dollar (Sunday September 13): approximately 152 yen per dollar ▲ yen strengthened from ~153 mid-weekThe yen's move from 156 (early September) to 152 reflects the US-Japan rate spread narrowing: BOJ's continued normalization signals alongside the Fed's approaching 82%-probable hike. At 152, Japanese exporters face meaningful earnings-revision pressure. The yen-carry trade's attractiveness continues to decline. No new BOJ commentary during the FOMC quiet-period equivalent window. Wednesday's Fed decision and dot plot will determine whether the dollar strengthens post-hike (yen to 155+) or weakens on a dovish dot plot (yen to 148-150).
Bottom line

Asian markets ended the week risk-off: Nikkei -1.93% on yen strength to 152, Hang Seng -0.60% on Brent-at-$104 energy costs. Monday's Asian session opens with the FOMC meeting starting in the US and Oman's GCC meeting - the first regional diplomatic signal since Brent crossed $104. Wednesday's dot plot is the yen's primary US-session catalyst.

Monday's floor activity on the ethics divestiture compromise is the last signal before the vote. At 20% passage odds, failure is the majority probability. A pass: XRP, ETH, and BTC regulation clarity in US law. A fail: no crypto legislative calendar before 2027 elections. The vote either clears 60 or fails. No middle ground.
Wednesday September 16: FOMC decision at ~2:00 PM ET · dot plot and press conference at 2:30 PM ETAt 82% hike odds, a 25-bps hike to 3.75-4.00% is the market's majority expectation. The press conference at 2:30 PM ET is Chair Powell's opportunity to clarify the committee's forward guidance.
