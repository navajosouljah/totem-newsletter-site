Market Pulse • Monday, November 16, 2026

Stocks Post Best Week in Months. Your CD Rate Hinges on Tuesday.

The Quick Read

Bitcoin fell to $61,970 over the weekend before recovering near $65K early Monday, and Asia opened with the Nikkei down 2.4%. Everything traces to one mechanism: futures now price 64 basis points of Fed tightening over the next year, up from 37 at November’s start. That repricing drives the dollar higher, pressing gold, crypto, and international assets lower at once. That is the mechanism.

Tuesday decides the rate path. October Retail Sales at 8:30 AM ET is the week’s first major data point. A hot print lifts the 13% chance of a 50bp December hike; a soft number keeps 25bp at about 87%. Best 1-year CDs hold near 5.00%, and both outcomes put January CDs between 5.25% and 5.50% - making the case for waiting through December 9 before locking a term. Nvidia also reports Tuesday after the close.

The Fed’s media blackout starts November 25 - nine days out. Wednesday’s FOMC minutes at 2:00 PM ET show how close the committee came to signaling a larger October move. Anthropic prices Wednesday night and begins trading Thursday, two days after Nvidia, testing whether investors still pay up for AI after a 20% chipmaker drawdown. December 9 is 23 days away.
