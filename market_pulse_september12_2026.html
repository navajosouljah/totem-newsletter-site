Stocks Rebounded Friday. CPI Confirmed Tuesday's Rate Decision at 82% Odds.

The Quick Read

August CPI arrived Friday at +3.4% year-over-year and +0.4% month-over-month - the headline matched consensus exactly, but the core printed +0.3% month-over-month, one tick above the 0.2% forecast. At the same moment, Iranian state media reported Tehran would meet Gulf states in Oman to discuss the Strait of Hormuz - the first diplomatic signal of the conflict cycle. Brent fell 2.8% to $104.61 from Thursday's $108 cycle high; WTI held above $100.05. Stocks snapped their four-day losing streak: S&P 500 +0.86% to 7,656.85, Dow +509 points to 52,573.10, Nasdaq +0.96% to 26,332.83. The mechanism: oil's Oman-driven pullback carried the session; the hot core CPI moved hike odds to approximately 82% on CME FedWatch from 72% entering the print - still not enough to stop the relief rally.

Tuesday September 15 at approximately 2:00 PM ET is the FOMC decision with hike odds at approximately 82% entering the weekend. A 25-bps hike to 3.75-4.00% is now the near-consensus expectation. Top CD rates at 4.35% are positioned to move to 4.50-4.60% post-decision; the window to lock 4.35% today or Monday closes Tuesday morning as banks reprice ahead of the announcement. CLARITY Act cloture lands at 2:15 PM ET Tuesday - 15 minutes after the FOMC - with a revised 630-page bill released Thursday. Senate Republicans narrowed the bill's DeFi provisions and added CFTC registration requirements targeting non-decentralized protocols; passage still needs seven Democratic votes with none committed.

Beneath Tuesday: the September 16 pump window carries current $104 Brent to retail gas on the same day as the FOMC press conference and dot plot at 2:30 PM ET. The AAA national average sits at $4.31 Saturday morning - the September 16 reset targets $4.40-4.50. USDA cut the corn yield to 178.5 bushels per acre in Friday's WASDE - above the 178.1 trade consensus but well above Pro Farmer's 173.2 bpa floor. Bank of Japan and Bank of England both decide September 17.
