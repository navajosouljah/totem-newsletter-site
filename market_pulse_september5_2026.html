August Delivered 162K Jobs. Hike Odds Hit 53% and Yields Crossed 4.82%.

The Quick Read

August nonfarm payrolls came in at 162,000 - the strongest month since May and a decisive beat over the 53,000 consensus. Unemployment held at 4.1%. Wages rose 0.3% month-over-month. The mechanism is direct: CME FedWatch's September hike probability climbed from 49.4% Thursday to 52.6% Friday, the 10-year Treasury closed at 4.823% - crossing the 4.81% ceiling it had tested twice this week - and the dollar strengthened against most peers. Every rate-linked product from CDs to mortgages repriced toward the hike scenario before Friday's close.

What September 11 decides is the direction from here. August CPI at 8:30 AM ET and the September WASDE at 12:00 PM ET arrive on the same Friday - five days before the FOMC decision September 15-16. CPI captured a full month at $4+ gas and $91+ WTI crude, plus the first two weeks of Canada lumber tariff pass-through. A reading above 3.5% converts the 52.6% hike probability into a near-certainty. Below 3.2% and the pause case revives even with Friday's jobs beat on the board.

Hormuz is the variable Friday's print did not resolve. With 6 vessels per day transiting the Strait versus 85 normally, the supply disruption is real and accumulating. Bitcoin absorbed the jobs-print selloff from $82,200 to $79,463 on $174.6 million in Friday's ETF inflows - smaller than Thursday's record $730.8 million but with institutional demand continuing through the pullback. The CLARITY Act enters the September 8-10 Senate window with the ethics divestiture clause unresolved and four working days before the September 15 cloture attempt.
