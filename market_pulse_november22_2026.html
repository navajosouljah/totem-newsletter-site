Yields Fell to 4.92% Friday. Gas at $3.66 Is the Month’s Low.

The Quick Read

The AI trade’s seventh consecutive selloff sent the S&P 500 into correction territory Friday, with Nvidia’s 14% collapse on an SEC accounting probe and ANT’s post-IPO fade as the dual catalysts. The selloff’s secondary effect was the mechanism that matters: forced selling in equities and crypto ($2 billion in 24-hour liquidations) drove flight-to-safety bond buying that pulled the 10-year yield from 5.051% Thursday to 4.92% Friday - its sharpest single-session drop in three weeks. The AI trade is repricing from debut optimism to earnings scrutiny, and that repricing is producing lower bond yields as a byproduct.

Wednesday, November 25 at 8:30 AM ET brings PCE, Q3 GDP second estimate, Initial Claims, and the FOMC media blackout’s start - the last pre-blackout input before December 9. With 25bp at approximately 91–94% and 50bp at approximately 6–9%, PCE is the final reading that widens or narrows December’s tail before 14 days of silence. After Wednesday morning, no Fed communication reaches markets until December 9.

Iowa corn sits at 84% harvested, 11 points behind the 95% average, with Monday’s USDA Crop Progress at 3:00 PM ET showing this week’s pace before the Thanksgiving window narrows. Gas fell to $3.6594/gallon (AAA, Saturday) - the month’s low and 42 cents below the November 1 peak. Bitcoin hit approximately $55,300 Saturday before recovering to approximately $61,000 Sunday as leveraged short positions faced liquidations.
