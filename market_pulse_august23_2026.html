Sunday Weekend Edition • Sunday, August 23, 2026

Jackson Hole Opens Tomorrow With September Hike Odds at 55% - Up From Last Week's 31%. Nvidia Reports Wednesday Before Warsh's First Keynote Friday. Hormuz Day 10: No Framework, Brent Near $94.

Gold extended to $4,617 and Bitcoin holds $76,700 as the real-asset bid sustains into Warsh week. XRP at $1.46 with September 15 confirmed as the CLARITY Act Senate floor date.

The Quick Read

Bessent's escalated buyback vow couldn't hold the 30-year below 5.27% by Friday's close, and September hike odds have jumped from 31% last week to approximately 55% entering Jackson Hole. Three compounding forces drove the shift: Brent near $94 after ten days of Hormuz disruption compiling an August CPI energy contribution above prior consensus, Walmart's worst comparable-store sales in six years confirming consumer deceleration at the trade-down anchor, and the 30-year Treasury's refusal to compress despite active buyback intervention. The three FOMC dissenters who voted for an immediate hike in July now hold ten additional days of sequential energy-and-consumer evidence heading into Warsh's first keynote. That is the whole mechanism.

This week decides September before the September data arrives. USDA crop progress lands Monday - 60% good-to-excellent corn entering the report. Nvidia reports Wednesday evening; consensus is $93-95 billion in Q2 revenue, with Q3 hyperscaler capex guidance the more important half. Warsh delivers his Jackson Hole keynote Friday at 10am ET, 19 days before the FOMC. At 55% hike odds, his framing choices are the most consequential Fed communication since the Hormuz crisis began - emphasize demand destruction and hold odds recover toward 40%; validate the dissenters' sequential-evidence case and September hike probability exceeds 70%.

The structural pressure the market hasn't fully priced: September 15 is simultaneously the FOMC's first day and the confirmed Senate floor date for the CLARITY Act, which would reclassify XRP, Solana, and Dogecoin as CFTC commodities rather than SEC securities. XRP's Friday surge to $1.42 extended to $1.46 Sunday as traders priced the legislative binary into the weekend. A CLARITY Act passage and a Fed rate decision on the same calendar day would be the most concentrated single-day regulatory and monetary event in this cycle.

How Sunday Opens

  • What 55% September hike odds mean for Warsh's Jackson Hole posture. At 55% hike odds - nearly double last week's 31% - Warsh arrives at his first keynote already holding the most market-priced September probability since before the July hold. Historically, when the market enters Jackson Hole with a hike partially priced, the asymmetry runs against the hawkish speaker: confirming what 55% already prices moves yields modestly, while a dovish surprise unwinds a half-positioned trade and produces a larger price move. Warsh's most powerful option is not hawkish confirmation - it is a conditional framework that specifies exactly what data combination changes the September calculus, which preserves his credibility with the three dissenters while giving the market a legible path to a hold. A conditional framing holds September odds near 55% but reduces the tail risk of a violent surprise in either direction. The rate market enters Friday's keynote with more binary asymmetry than any recent Jackson Hole.
  • Nvidia enters Wednesday's earnings as the S&P's only available catalyst before Warsh speaks Friday. The S&P closed at 7,674 Friday - its second consecutive weekly loss from approximately 7,785 Monday open (-1.4%). Nvidia management guided $91 billion in Q2 revenue; Wall Street consensus is $93-95 billion. The Q3 guidance on hyperscaler AI capex is the more important half: if Microsoft, Google, Meta, and Amazon are maintaining or accelerating AI infrastructure spend at 5.27%/4.70% rates, the Nasdaq's two-week derating narrative reverses before Warsh speaks. If Nvidia guides cautiously on Q3 - acknowledging rate sensitivity in hyperscaler capex decisions - the derating extends through Jackson Hole and Warsh inherits a market that has absorbed three consecutive down weeks. The equity market's path into Jackson Hole is more dependent on Nvidia's Wednesday guidance than on any single rate communication Warsh is likely to offer on Friday.
  • Gold at $4,617 with the 30-year rising: the real-yield correlation is fracturing. Gold extended to $4,617 Sunday while the 30-year Treasury closed Friday at approximately 5.27% - a combination that contradicts the standard real-yield model, where rising real yields suppress gold by raising the opportunity cost of holding a non-yielding asset. That the model is failing means institutional demand at this level is not yield-sensitive - it is duration-risk sensitive. With US debt past $40 trillion and the buyback program's November 4 expiration as the forward risk, institutions are building gold positions as a hedge against the rate environment after the buyback program ends, not against the current managed rate. The $4,600-$4,650 range established this week is the market's pricing of that forward duration risk - a risk no rate forecast is currently incorporating.
  • Hormuz Day 10 closes without a confirming signal, and Monday's oil open is the verdict on Thursday's report. Ten days have passed since the MOU expiry on August 13. CBSNews reported Thursday evening that Iran and Oman were "getting close" to a framework - but no official statement from Oman's foreign ministry, Iranian state media, or the US State Department confirmed or qualified that characterization through Sunday morning. In prior Oman-mediated negotiations, Oman's foreign ministry typically issues a public readout within 48-72 hours of substantive progress. That readout has not arrived. Monday's oil market open is the first market price discovery event since Thursday's report - Brent holding above $94 at Monday's open is functionally the market concluding that Friday-Saturday produced no framework progress, and that Jackson Hole week begins against full Hormuz pricing with Warsh's keynote as its macro backdrop.
  • CLARITY Act: September 15 concurrent with FOMC, and the ethics dispute remains unsolved with three weeks left. The CLARITY Act Senate floor vote on or around September 15 places XRP's regulatory binary concurrent with the FOMC's first day. The bill would reclassify XRP, Solana, and Dogecoin as CFTC-regulated commodities - ending the SEC enforcement exposure that has followed XRP since the 2023 lawsuit. XRP extended Friday's 15% surge to $1.46 Sunday - a directional divergence from BTC's weekend pullback to $76,700 and ETH's decline to $2,386 that confirms XRP is trading as a legislative binary, not a correlated crypto asset. The bipartisan dispute over whether officials holding more than $1 million in digital assets must divest before regulatory votes remains the single unresolved obstacle. Polymarket passage odds stand at approximately 27%. September is the bill's final realistic window before November midterms occupy the calendar. Three working weeks remain to reach a compromise the Senate has not been able to reach in three months of negotiation.
