Savers Get a Raise Today. FOMC and CLARITY Act Vote This Afternoon.

The Quick Read

The 10-year Treasury crossed 5.041% Tuesday morning - its highest since 2007 - and the Federal Reserve is near-certain (87-92% CME FedWatch odds) to deliver a 25-basis-point hike at approximately 2:00 PM ET today, the first hike since July 2023. August CPI held at 3.4% year-over-year (+0.4% month-over-month), matching consensus. Brent at $107.46 on Saudi pipeline disruption kept inflation risk intact through the weekend. The mechanism: a 25-bps move lifts the fed funds target to 3.75-4.00%, pushes HYSA rates toward 4.35-4.45% within weeks, and adds approximately 25 basis points to the 22.15% average card APR for the 13.12% of balances already 90 days past due.

Fifteen minutes after the FOMC - at 2:15 PM ET - the Senate votes cloture on the CLARITY Act. The rate hike at 87-92% is near-certain; CLARITY Act passage at roughly 20-25% odds is not. The bill needs 60 votes; Republicans hold 53 seats, and the ethics divestiture clause - barring officials from issuing digital assets and requiring blind trusts for existing holdings - remains unresolved. If cloture fails today, the Senate's window closes until 2027. Bitcoin is pricing that uncertainty: down 1.1% to $76,837, holding above $75,000 but $4,329 below its September 4 peak of $81,166.

A third layer opened Monday. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published "We Must Pace the Frontier" over the weekend - a call to slow AI development - cosigned by OpenAI's Sam Altman, Google DeepMind's Demis Hassabis, and xAI's Elon Musk. The Nasdaq fell 0.56% Monday; tech futures are down another 0.7% pre-market Tuesday. Brent at $107 and the 10-year at 5% are the macro headwind today. Monday's crop progress brought the one constructive surprise: corn G/E rose to 57% from 56%, a one-point rebound after four weeks of declines, and the September 11 WASDE corn yield cut came in smaller than expected at 178.5 bpa.
