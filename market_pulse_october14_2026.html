September CPI at 8:30 AM ET Today. Top 1-Year CDs Pay 4.75%.

The Quick Read

September CPI arrives at 8:30 AM ET today - the last inflation print before the October 27-28 FOMC meeting and the number that moves December hike probability off its current 78%. Consensus calls for 3.7% headline, up from August's 3.4%, with core steady at 2.4% year-over-year. Energy drove the headline: Brent climbed more than 14% in September and gasoline surged over 7% that month. The mechanism is straightforward - a fuel-driven headline pop the Fed will weigh against core stability. That calculus is what the 8:30 AM ET number resolves. Morgan Stanley and Bank of America also report Q3 earnings before the bell, closing out major-bank earnings week.

The October 28 FOMC is priced at 82% hold regardless of today's print - the meeting is too close to the data to pivot. December is the live question. A print at or above 3.8% pushes December hike probability meaningfully above 78% and toward near-certain; a miss below 3.3% would be the first downside inflation surprise of 2026, the reading that could begin to soften the December calculation. Freddie Mac publishes its next weekly mortgage survey Thursday; the 10-year's reaction to this morning's CPI is exactly what that survey will reflect.

Top 1-year CDs are available at 4.75% APY this morning. That window is open today - the post-CPI rate repricing does not happen overnight. The USDA Crop Progress report published Monday confirmed harvest accelerating as dry conditions returned to the Midwest: corn at 33% harvested, soybeans at 46%, both running ahead of their respective five-year averages for the first time this season.
