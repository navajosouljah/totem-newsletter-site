4.75% CDs Hold as Yields Ease. Meta Raises AI Budget to $145B.

The Quick Read

Meta posted $64.6 billion in Q3 revenue - up 26% - and raised its AI capital expenditure guidance to $145 billion for 2026, the largest single-company AI spending commitment on record. Tesla beat EPS at $0.48 (consensus $0.43) but cut its own capital expenditure forecast to $20 billion from more than $25 billion, with automotive revenue down 3% year over year and free cash flow plunging 62% to $1.53 billion. The split is the mechanism: the market is separating companies that are accelerating AI investment from those trimming it - and it is pricing that gap tonight. Meta rose 2.5% after hours. Tesla fell 3%. Nasdaq futures are down 0.7% pre-market Thursday on the capex-cut signal.

Thursday carries the last pre-FOMC data: jobless claims for the week ending October 18 arrive at 8:30 AM ET (prior 199,000, consensus ~200,000) and Freddie Mac's PMMS publishes at noon ET. The 10-year Treasury fell 6 basis points overnight to approximately 5.08% as risk appetite pulled back on Tesla's earnings - the largest single-week yield retreat in three weeks. A 5.08% close through Wednesday would give Thursday's PMMS a genuine shot at the first improvement below 7.40% since September.

The slower-building story: Brent crude rose 2.6% to $111.64 early Thursday on fresh Houthi attacks on Saudi facilities - the fourth supply-disruption event in two weeks. Asian markets reacted to the Tesla capex cut with their sharpest session since March: Nikkei down 2.65%, Hang Seng down 3.2%. The USD/JPY holds near 158.1, 1.9 points from the Ministry of Finance's prior 160 intervention threshold. USDA crop progress for the critical October 19-25 harvest window arrives Monday, October 26.
