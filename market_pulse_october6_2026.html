Nasdaq Set a Record High. Services Held at 54.9 – FOMC Minutes Arrive Tomorrow.

The Quick Read

Monday’s Nasdaq closed at a fresh all-time high of 27,477.31 despite the ISM Services Prices Index hitting 74.0 percent – its highest reading since July 2022. The composite came in at 54.9 (consensus 55.1, prior 55.4), the 27th consecutive month of services expansion, and the Employment Index returned to expansion at 50.1 for the first time in three months. That is the mechanism: services expansion held and equities led, while inflation pressure inside the sector pushed yields upward. The 10-year settled near 5.28%, the 2-year at 4.823%, and October 28 hike odds remained near 20 percent – effectively priced out.

Wednesday’s FOMC minutes at 2:00 PM ET are now the week’s sharpest catalyst, carrying fresh context the September 15-16 committee did not have. The dot plot placed the median 2026 terminal rate at 4.10 percent, with 16 of 18 participants expecting one more hike this year. The minutes will show how much committee support exists beyond the median – and whether any faction cited services inflation specifically when framing December. October 28 hike odds near 20 percent price a near-certain hold; December is the consensus next-action scenario. Consumer Credit (August) releases Wednesday at 3:00 PM ET as a secondary data point on the household side.

Monday’s USDA Crop Progress report showed both corn and soybean harvests running behind the five-year average as of October 4. Corn reached 23 percent harvested versus a 27 percent five-year average; soybeans at 25 percent. A wet second half of September slowed Corn Belt field work. Corn good-to-excellent ratings fell 3 points to 54 percent. Dry weather forecast across the Corn Belt this week should accelerate progress toward the record WASDE yields. Asian markets extended Monday’s gains: Nikkei +0.90% to 70,576, Hang Seng +0.71% to 24,210. Curated from ISM · BLS · CME FedWatch · USDA · Bloomberg · CoinMarketCap
