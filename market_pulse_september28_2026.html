Bitcoin ETF Floor Holds at $999M. PCE Wednesday Decides the CD Ceiling.

The Quick Read

Trump's rejection of Iran's Hormuz proposal Saturday reset Brent's risk premium above $106 Monday - reversing last week's $3-4 easing and removing the near-term diplomatic off-ramp. October 28 FOMC hike odds held at approximately 73-76% (CME FedWatch), back to their Thursday position before Iran's Friday proposal. Asian markets opened mixed: Nikkei -0.1% to 66,333, Hang Seng +0.7% to 24,684, with South Korea's Kospi falling 2.3% to 6,916. The mechanism: three weeks of Brent above $104 feeds directly into the energy-cost component of August PCE's measurement window (through August 31), and Trump's rejection removes the diplomatic path that might have softened it entering October.

Wednesday's August PCE (8:30 AM ET) is the week's only remaining probability pivot for October 28. July PCE ran 3.7% YoY headline and 3.3% YoY core. Above 2.5% YoY pushes October odds toward 80%, confirming the wait for a post-hike 4.90-5.10% CD ceiling. Only below 2.0% pulls October odds below 50% and makes locking 4.95% today defensible - a narrower path now that Brent's structural $100+ floor is reinstated. Friday's September payrolls (8:30 AM ET) closes the Q3 labor picture and is the week's second major decision point.

Bitcoin ETF inflows hit $999M Monday - up from $433M Friday and the strongest single-day institutional read since the CLARITY Act failed 50-49 on September 15. The $2B September institutional floor held through last week's near-$108 Brent, 5.22% 10-year stress session and strengthened entering this week. The USDA Crop Progress report (week ending September 27) publishes today at 3:00 PM ET - the first post-harvest-ramp read of the season. Gas at $4.49 nationally keeps the mid-October $4.95-$5.10 pump window on track, and credit card delinquencies near 13% at 22.40% APR continue building into Q4.
