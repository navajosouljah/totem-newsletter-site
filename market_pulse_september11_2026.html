Savers See 72% Odds of a Rate Lift. CPI This Morning Decides.

The Quick Read

PPI arrived Thursday at +0.4% month-over-month and +5.4% year-over-year - the hottest producer-price reading of this conflict cycle, driven by energy. The 10-year Treasury climbed to 4.97%, the highest level since October 2023. September 15 hike odds on CME FedWatch moved to 72% from Thursday morning's 63% - nine percentage points in a single session. Brent crude reached a new cycle high near $108 in Thursday's session before settling. That is the full mechanism: producers absorbed August's crude spike in input costs, the 10-year priced the consumer pass-through ahead of today's data, and the market's rate decision already stands at 72% before the Bureau of Labor Statistics opens its doors this morning.

August CPI arrives at 8:30 AM ET - the Fed's final inflation reading before the September 15 FOMC decision. Consensus: +3.4% year-over-year, +0.4% month-over-month. At 3.4% or above: the 25-bps hike to 3.75-4.00% is confirmed, and top CD rates already at 4.35% are priced to move higher post-decision. Below 3.0%: the only data path that revives the pause case before the quiet period closes September 15. WASDE at noon ET brings USDA's first formal corn yield revision - trade consensus expects 178.1 bushels per acre from the prior ~180.7 estimate.

Beneath today's data: the pump window opening September 16 carries current $106 Brent to retail gas on the same day as the FOMC press conference and dot plot. The bilateral lumber tariff stack (US 50% softwood, Canada counter-tariffs now on Day 4) deepens housing costs ahead of August starts data. CLARITY Act arrives Monday at its final Senate session - cloture Tuesday at 2:15 PM ET - with passage odds near 16% on Polymarket after three working days without movement on the ethics impasse.
