Home Depot Beats Estimates. CD Savers' December Window Is 22 Days Out.

The Quick Read

Consumer spending showed up before the bell. Home Depot reported $43.4 billion in Q3 revenue - beating the $42.8 billion estimate - with comparable sales up 2.2% and online orders up 13% for the sixth straight double-digit quarter. That sets the morning frame for October Retail Sales at 8:30 AM ET: a consumer still spending on home projects argues for the +0.2% consensus or better. The mechanism: spending fuels inflation, inflation locks in December’s hike, each hike lifts CD rates and presses crypto. That is the mechanism.

Retail Sales decides the December rate calculus. CME FedWatch already shifted from Friday’s 87%/13% split to approximately 95% for 25bp and just 5% for 50bp - a print above +0.4% risks reopening the larger-hike debate, while a miss below zero would push yields lower and hand CD savers a weaker December target. The Fed’s blackout starts November 25, eight days from now. Nvidia reports after today’s close with consensus at $108.85 billion in revenue - its first quarter without Jensen Huang.

Anthropic prices its IPO Wednesday evening at $70–$78 per share and begins trading Thursday on Nasdaq as ANT - at a valuation between $917 billion and $1.4 trillion. The timing matters: institutional buyers pulled $2.12 billion from spot Bitcoin ETFs this month while the Nasdaq lost 11% from its October 6 record. How buyers respond to ANT on Thursday tests the AI sector’s real appetite - not just Nvidia’s print.
