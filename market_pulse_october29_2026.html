Microsoft Azure Beats 48% Growth. Fed Holds as GDP and PCE Print at 8:30 AM ET.

The Quick Read

The Fed held at 3.75–4.00% Wednesday with a 10-2 vote - Governors Cook and Hammack both dissented for a 25-basis-point hike - and Chair Warsh declined forward guidance: “I don’t believe in it, absent exigent circumstances.” Alphabet then missed Q3 earnings for the first time in four years, with negative free cash flow of $9.07 billion and soft search advertising, falling over 10% in after-hours trading. Microsoft beat on every line - revenue $92.28 billion, Azure up 48% - rising 3% after hours. The split confirms what SK Hynix’s record set up Wednesday morning: AI capital spending is intact at hyperscalers executing it; the risk is in overbuilding at those that are not. That is the mechanism Thursday opens pricing.

Q3 GDP advance, September PCE, and weekly jobless claims land simultaneously at 8:30 AM ET this morning - six hours from now at time of writing. Q3 is the first full quarter under elevated rates; PCE is the Fed’s preferred inflation gauge; claims are the first labor read post-FOMC. Consensus puts Q3 GDP at 3.2% (Atlanta Fed GDPNow 3.3%), September PCE headline at 3.7%, and claims at approximately 198–199K. A GDP beat above 3.2% with hot PCE would push December’s 83–85% hike odds higher and reprice the 2-year immediately. The ECB also decides today.

Iowa corn stands at 47% harvested - 20 points behind the five-year average of 67% - with one week before northern corn belt freeze risk rises materially. The yen holds near 157.80 per dollar, 2.2 points from the Ministry of Finance’s 160 intervention threshold, with the BoJ deciding Friday. Friday also brings the Q3 Employment Cost Index at 8:30 AM ET - the wage-inflation input that feeds directly into PCE forecasts. Canada’s softwood lumber tariff enters Day 79.
