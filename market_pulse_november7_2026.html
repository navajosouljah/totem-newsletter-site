The Quick Read

Friday’s October NFP came in at 165,000 – more than double the 65,000 consensus and well above ADP’s 86,000 – and unemployment fell to 4.1%, unwinding Thursday’s hold-scenario positioning in a single report. The 10-year climbed from Thursday’s flight-to-quality low of approximately 4.65% back to approximately 4.87% as December hike odds moved from 67% to approximately 75%. The Nasdaq recovered 1.09% from Thursday’s AI sell-off; the Dow slipped 0.46% as energy and financial components faced the higher-rate headwind. That repricing – a 165,000 payroll beat pushing hike odds back toward three-in-four – is the full mechanism entering the weekend.

Monday’s November 9 USDA Crop Progress is the Iowa corn harvest read; freeze risk arrived this weekend, and Iowa stood at 66% harvested as of November 1 – 13 points behind the five-year average. The threshold is 75% by November 9: below that with freeze materializing, December corn futures reprice upward at Monday’s open. Tuesday’s November 10 CPI at 8:30 AM ET arrives 29 days before the December 9 FOMC; a re-acceleration from October’s reading would push hike odds above 85%.

Gas wave #3 – locked from October 28’s $115.32 Brent – arrives at retail stations November 8–12 regardless of Friday’s $98.32 close. CD top rates hold at 4.65% APY; with December at approximately 75%, waiting through December 9 retains two-in-three upside on a 4.90% January rate.
