Bitcoin Cleared $81K This Weekend. CDs at 4.50% APY - Two-Year Best.

The Quick Read

Bitcoin broke through $81,000 this weekend as ETF flows reversed sharply positive - IBIT recorded $246 million in net inflows Saturday, total Bitcoin ETF flows came in at $223 million net positive, and BTC stands at approximately $81,260 Sunday morning, up nearly 5% from Friday's close near $77,000-78,000. The institutional floor above $75,000 that held through Tuesday's Fed hike and Thursday's CLARITY failure is now $81,000 - the mechanism: institutions accumulate on sentiment dips because the ETF structure provides consistent demand regardless of legislative outcome, and the rate hike is fully priced.

PCE on Friday September 26 is what this week decides. Core PCE at or above +0.3% month over month locks in the November FOMC hike (currently at 60-65% odds); below +0.2% begins to soften the probability. Before that: Monday September 21 USDA crop progress (3:00 PM ET) delivers the first full harvest-week read - corn expected to advance from 8% to approximately 13-15% harvested. Conference Board consumer confidence on Tuesday September 23 is the first post-hike household sentiment read (August baseline: 103.3).

Two slow pressures define the week. Savers hold the best 1-year CD window in two years at 4.50% APY - the dot plot's 12-of-18 November signal means a second ceiling of 4.60-4.75% is ahead, making the lock-or-wait decision live for another seven days. On the other side: card APRs moved to approximately 22.40% against a 13.12% delinquency rate. Gas fell to $4.44 today as Saudi Arabia's partial pipeline restart tracks ahead of the six-week full-capacity estimate - the first household energy relief since September 11.

The August baseline was 103.3. A reading above 100 suggests consumers absorbed the September catalysts without deterioration; below 95 would signal the compound effects of 22.40% card APRs, $4.44 gas, and 13.12% delinquency are weighing on forward expectations.
