Nvidia Beats by $7 Billion. Gold at a 14-Month Low.

The Quick Read

Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in fiscal Q3 revenue - beating the $108.85 billion estimate by nearly $7 billion - with EPS of $2.68 against $2.47 consensus, Q4 guidance of $130 billion, and a $150 billion buyback addition. That settles the morning’s central question: AI capital spending did not retreat after Jensen Huang’s departure. The mechanism: Nvidia’s $107.6 billion data center beat validates every major cloud buyer’s capex plan - Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta - removing the AI-spending-pullback fear that drove the Nasdaq 11% below its October 6 record. The selloff priced in a doubt yesterday’s earnings just answered. Futures are higher Wednesday morning.

Two events finish the week’s rate picture today. FOMC minutes land at 2:00 PM ET - the last major rate input before the November 25 media blackout, now 7 days away. CME FedWatch already prices 25bp at approximately 97% and 50bp at approximately 3%. Yesterday’s October Retail Sales came in at exactly +0.2% month over month, in line with consensus; September was revised up to +0.8%. With the data settled, the minutes are what determines whether December is fully locked before the blackout.

Anthropic prices its IPO tonight at $70–$78 per share - 6x oversubscribed and likely above range per CNBC sources - for a Thursday Nasdaq debut as ANT. That test of institutional AI appetite arrives against two pressures: gold fell to approximately $3,787/oz, a 14-month low, as the stronger December-certainty dollar pressed metals; and spot Bitcoin ETFs shed $240 million on Tuesday alone, pushing November’s total outflows to approximately $1.52 billion.
