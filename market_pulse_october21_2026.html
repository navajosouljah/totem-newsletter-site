4.75% CDs Hold as Yields Ease. Tesla and Meta Report Tonight.

The Quick Read

Housing starts confirmed the builder stress signal yesterday: 1.221 million SAAR, down 5.2% from August and the second-lowest rate in 25 months. Seven consecutive weeks of 7.40% mortgage rates, a $6,000 per-home softwood tariff headwind, and 12.9% card delinquency have compressed the qualified-buyer pool to its narrowest since early 2024. Tonight cuts through the noise: Texas Instruments at 3:30 PM CT and Tesla and Meta after close deliver the three answers the October Nasdaq selloff demanded - semiconductor revenue, EV demand under elevated financing costs, and AI-infrastructure return-on-spend rationale. That is the whole mechanism.

TI's Q3 consensus is $5.92 billion against Q2's $5.46 billion; Q4 guidance above $6.0 billion confirms the semiconductor-cycle recovery the Nasdaq has been pricing in uncertainty around. Tesla's 486,532 Q3 deliveries beat the 461,974 consensus - but the stock watches one number tonight: automotive gross margin excluding regulatory credits. A reading above 15% confirms the cost-reduction cycle is holding under $110 Brent and 7.40% financing rates. Meta's guidance on its $130-145 billion AI capex plan needs spending-rationale language that sounds like return on investment, not escalation.

The 10-year at 5.12% Wednesday morning - slightly off Tuesday's 5.15% on Iran deal signals that faded - gives Thursday's Freddie Mac PMMS a tight path to the first improvement since September. Gas at $4.2786 (AAA Wednesday) predates the Friday crude spike; pump prices rise approximately October 26-31. XRP gained 2% Wednesday morning while institutional ETF flows remained at zero for the seventh straight session - the legislative pause is intact, and only tonight's results, not price, can shift it.
