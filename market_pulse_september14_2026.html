Savers' Rate Answer Arrives Wednesday. AI Pacing Call Hits Tech.

The Quick Read

The S&P 500 opens Monday at 7,656.98 with Nasdaq 100 futures down 1.72% after AI founders' pacing consensus landed over the weekend - Amodei (Anthropic), Altman (OpenAI), and Musk endorsed slowing AI development, sending NVIDIA down 2% and chip names down 5-6% in pre-market. The mechanism: the AI-infrastructure spending thesis, which absorbed Friday's 3.4% CPI without a rout, faces a founders-level challenge from the names that built it. SoftBank fell 13.2% in Tokyo's session as both that chip exposure and yen strength compounded.

Wednesday's FOMC decision settles the CD rate question. At 85.5% hike odds - updated after Friday's PPI came in at 5.4% - the 25-bps hike to 3.75-4.00% is the market's near-certainty entering today. Banks already pre-moved top 1-year CDs to 4.60%, 30 basis points above where they stood two weeks ago. Tomorrow settles the crypto regulation question: CLARITY Act cloture is Tuesday September 15 at 2:15 PM ET at 33% passage odds - up from 20% after Trump reportedly accepted the ethics divestiture provision over the weekend. XRP gained 2% to approximately $1.38-1.39 on that news.

Underneath both decisions: Brent at $104.61 after the Saudi East-West pipeline shutdown (drone attacks September 10-11) removed approximately 4-5 million barrels per day - and simultaneously disrupted both remaining Hormuz bypass routes. Credit card delinquencies hold at 13.12% (15-year high) as the 22.15% average APR faces an 85.5%-probable 25-bps increase Wednesday. Tonight's USDA Crop Progress (week ending September 14) is the first harvest-week G/E reading after Friday's WASDE cut corn to 178.5 bpa.
