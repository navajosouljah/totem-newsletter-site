Market Pulse • Thursday, September 3, 2026

Gold at $4,461, Futures Hold. Tomorrow's Jobs Report Is One Day Away.

The Quick Read

Thursday opens with two data prints before Friday's jobs report. Jobless claims arrive at 8:30 AM ET as the first unofficial read on August labor. The Freddie Mac PMMS drops at noon ET - likely 6.68 to 6.74% based on this week's 4.79% ten-year - the first official mortgage reading after yields crossed 4.75%. The September 15 decision holds at 66% odds. A Friday print under 100,000 moves it to 45-50%; above 150,000 with 3.5% wages pushes past 70%.

Gold climbed 1.8% to $4,461 before the New York open, not because Iran declared Hormuz closed or struck US facilities in Jordan, Kuwait, and Bahrain - Brent is near flat at $95 - but because the 10-year held below 4.81%. Rate path first, geopolitics second. Futures point to a quiet open: S&P up 0.1%, Nasdaq flat. Asia was mixed, with the Hang Seng flat and Tokyo near 64,300.

Corn futures held near $5.21, and today's USDA Drought Monitor updates the condition picture ahead of the September 11 WASDE. Bitcoin held near $77,400. The CLARITY Act sits at 11 working days to September 15 cloture, the Senate in session today and tomorrow before Labor Day. The ethics divestiture clause is still the unresolved block.
